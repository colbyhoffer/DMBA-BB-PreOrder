Questions

What if a design doesn't get 6 orders?
It gets dropped from the order. Anyone who picked it will be offered a swap to another design, or can just skip it. No charge either way.
What if we don't reach 24 hats total?
The order doesn't go in and nobody pays anything. We'll either extend the window or call it.
When do I pay?
After the window closes and the counts are confirmed. You'll get a message with the exact amount and where to send it. The order isn't placed until everyone has paid.
What sizes are these?
One size, adjustable snapback closure on all four styles.
Can I order more than one?
Yes. Any mix of designs, up to 5 of each.
When do hats arrive?
Branded Bills typically takes a few weeks to produce after the order is placed. Colby will share updates by email.
